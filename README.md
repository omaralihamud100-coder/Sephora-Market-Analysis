# Sephora Market Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes over 8,000 Sephora beauty products to identify pricing trends, customer demand, and business opportunities in the U.S. cosmetics market.

## Key Insights
- Skincare (2,351 products) and Makeup (2,328 products) dominate the market
- Most high-demand products are priced between $15–$40
- Top products include mascara, blush, lip care, and foundation
- No strong relationship between price and rating

## Top Products
- Tattoo Liner (KVD Beauty) – 21,281 reviews  
- Better Than Sex Mascara (Too Faced) – 18,610 reviews  
- Soft Pinch Liquid Blush (Rare Beauty) – 1.4M loves

## Goal
Identify profitable product opportunities for launching a cosmetics e-commerce business.
